The baker behind the folk heart

A Slovak kitchen, a family recipe book, and a Queensland address.

  1. Home

It started with a walnut grinder

I grew up in a house where the oven was never really off. My mum always baked on the weekends, and our job as kids was to turn the handle of her cast-iron walnut grinder until our arms ached. She kept her recipes in her old recipe book, written in pencil, with butter fingerprints on the page for bábovka.

That recipe book came with me to Australia.

Why the folk heart

When I miss Slovakia, the blue flowers and red hearts remind me of home and the traditions I grew up with. Keeping these folk patterns in my baking is a way to stay close to my roots and carry those traditions on. I love sharing a little piece of that feeling with everyone who enjoys my baking.

How I bake now

Out of my own kitchen on the Gold Coast, in small batches, to order. I do not freeze anything and I do not use premixes or preservatives — which means I need notice, and it means I can only take so many orders in a week.

For many years, I have been baking for the Czechoslovakian Club in Brisbane, and seeing people enjoy my cakes makes me happy. There is something about a Walnut strudel or Drunken Izidor that takes everyone down their own memory lane. I have made it my purpose to bring traditional flavours back to those who hold these memories.

Where to find Katka. Orders are collected from the Gold Coast, QLD. Katka will send you the pickup address and a time once your order is confirmed.
