What is collected

When you send an enquiry through this website, Katka’s Kitchen receives the name, email address, phone number, date and message you typed into the form. Nothing else is collected, and there is no account to create.

How it is used

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and make and hand over your order. Your details are not sold, rented or shared with anyone for marketing. You will not be added to a mailing list unless you ask to be.

Where it is stored

Enquiries arrive as email at info@katkaskitchen.com.au and are kept only as long as needed for the order and ordinary business records.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Visitor numbers are counted in aggregate so Katka can see which pages are useful; no individual visitor is identified and no profile is built.

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google’s servers to fetch them.

Your choices

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email info@katkaskitchen.com.au and Katka will sort it out.
