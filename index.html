Ahoj, I’m Katka

A Slovak kitchen that moved to Queensland

I learned to bake standing on a stool next to my grandmother, turning the handle of her walnut grinder. When we moved to the Gold Coast I brought her recipe notebook with me — the one with butter fingerprints on the page for medovník.

Now I bake from my own kitchen here, for Slovak and Czech families who miss the taste of home, and for Australian neighbours who had never tried a honey cake until someone handed them a slice.
