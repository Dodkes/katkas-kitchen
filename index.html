Ahoj, I’m Katarina but you can call me Katka

A Slovak kitchen that moved to Queensland

I learned to bake standing on a stool next to my mum, turning the handle of her walnut grinder. She would bake for us every weekend, and I remember mixing eggs with icing sugar using a wooden spoon until my hand hurt. I was amazed that adding a few ingredients together could create such delicious treats.

Now I bake from my own kitchen here, for Slovak and Czech families who miss the taste of home, and for Australian neighbours who had never tried a honey cake until someone handed them a slice.
