Ordering, allergens & prices

The practical things worth knowing before you order. If your question is not here, just ask.

How far ahead should I order?

For celebration cakes, two weeks is comfortable and one week is usually fine. Dessert boxes and trays need two to three days. Bread and yeast pastries need to be ordered by Thursday for a weekend pickup. Weddings are booked six to nine months ahead for peak season.

Always ask about short notice — if Katka has room, she will squeeze you in.

Where are you based, and how do I collect my order?

Katka bakes from her own kitchen on the Gold Coast, Queensland. Orders are collected in person. Once your order is confirmed she will send you the pickup address and arrange a time that suits.

Can you make gluten free, dairy free or nut free versions?

Some things, yes — and some things genuinely do not work. Ask about the specific item.

Be aware that everything is baked in one home kitchen that handles wheat, eggs, dairy, walnuts, hazelnuts, almonds and poppy seed daily. Katka cannot guarantee any item is free of traces. If an allergy is severe, she will tell you honestly that her kitchen is not the right choice.

How much does a cake cost?

A 6″ buttercream cake serving eight to ten starts at $95; an 8″ serving sixteen to twenty starts at $135. Two-tier wedding cakes start at $450. A mixed box of six desserts is $38.

Detailed modelling, fresh flowers, multiple fillings and tiered builds all move the price. Send Katka your details and she will quote exactly.

What is medovník?

It is the best-known cake in Slovakia: six or seven thin baked honey layers, filled with a tangy sour cream and caramel filling, then left in the fridge for two days so the layers soften. It is not a sponge and it is not gingerbread — it is its own thing, and it is why most people come back.

Do you take wedding tastings?

Yes. A tasting box for two is $45 and includes six flavours with notes on each. If you go ahead and book, the $45 comes off your final invoice.

How do I pay, and do you need a deposit?

Bank transfer or card. A 30% deposit confirms your date and the balance is due on pickup. For weddings the balance is due two weeks before the event.

Can you copy a cake I have seen online?

Katka is happy to work from a photograph as a starting point, and will tell you what will and will not survive a Queensland summer afternoon. She will not pass off another baker’s work as her own design, so expect her version rather than an exact copy.

How long will my cake keep?

Buttercream cakes are best within three days, kept in the fridge and brought out an hour before serving. Medovník is actually better on day two or three. Custard-based desserts — kremeš, veterníky — should be eaten the same day. Yeast pastries are best the day they are baked, and freeze well after that.

Do you cater for markets, cafes or offices?

Small wholesale and regular office orders are possible depending on the week. Email Katka with quantities and how often, and she will be realistic about what a one-person kitchen can sustain.
