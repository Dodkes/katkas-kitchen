Ordering & Allergens

The practical things worth knowing before you order. If your question is not here, just ask.

How far ahead should I order?

Dessert boxes and trays need two to three days. Yeast pastries need to be ordered by Thursday for a weekend pickup.

Always ask about short notice — if Katka has room, she will squeeze you in.

Where are you based, and how do I collect my order?

Katka bakes from her own kitchen on the Gold Coast, Queensland. Orders are collected in person. Once your order is confirmed she will send you the pickup address and arrange a time that suits.

Can you make gluten free, dairy free or nut free versions?

Some things, yes — and some things genuinely do not work. Ask about the specific item.

Be aware that everything is baked in one home kitchen that handles wheat, eggs, dairy, walnuts, hazelnuts, almonds and poppy seed daily. Katka cannot guarantee any item is free of traces. If an allergy is severe, she will tell you honestly that her kitchen is not the right choice.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit is required for all orders. Payment can be made by bank transfer, card or cash.
