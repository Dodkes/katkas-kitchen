Slovak favourites

  • Walnut crescents orechové rohlíčky Buttery walnut biscuits shaped into crescents and dusted with icing sugar.
  • Linzer jam cookies linecké koláčiky Delicate sandwich biscuits filled with a layer of fruit jam.
  • Chocolate quark slices tvarohové rezy Soft chocolate sponge layered with a smooth, creamy quark filling.
  • Honey kremes medový krémeš Crisp pastry layered with honey-scented cream and custard.
  • Poppy seed slices makové rezy Tender cake slices with a rich, fragrant poppy seed filling.
  • Elephant tears slonie slzy Soft cocoa sponge rolled around banana and walnut cream, finished with chocolate.
  • Metre cake slices metrové rezy Alternating vanilla and cocoa sponge with buttercream, cut on an angle to reveal the stripes.
  • Rumba cake rumba zákusok Rich chocolate dessert with a rum flavour.
  • Mandarin slices mandarínkové rezy Light sponge and creamy filling dotted with juicy mandarin segments.
  • Drunken Izidor opitý Izidor Chocolate sponge with walnut and rum-flavoured filling, finished with chocolate icing.
  • Gerbeaud slices Žerbo rezy Layers of pastry with apricot jam and ground walnuts, topped with chocolate.
  • Lick-your-fingers cake oblíž prst The queen of Slovak desserts: cocoa sponge, a creamy custard layer and a meringue topping.
  • Miša slices miša rezy A traditional Slovak slice with cocoa sponge and a creamy ricotta layer, finished with chocolate.

Can’t find your favourite on the list? Ask Katka whether it is available.

Allergen information. Everything is baked in a home kitchen that regularly handles wheat, eggs, dairy, walnuts, hazelnuts, almonds and poppy seeds. Katka cannot guarantee that any item is free from traces of these allergens. Please mention any allergies when you enquire, and Katka will let you know whether an item is suitable.
