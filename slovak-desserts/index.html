Never tried these before?

Most of Katka’s Australian customers arrive having never heard of any of it. Here is the short version.

Medovník is a honey cake — thin baked layers, not sponge — with a tangy sour cream filling. It needs two days in the fridge before it is ready, which is why it cannot be made in a hurry.

Kremeš is the Central European vanilla slice: far more custard than you are expecting, between two sheets of puff pastry.

Punčové rezy are pink punch slices — sponge offcuts bound with jam and rum, glazed, and cut into squares. Every Slovak grandmother has a version.

If you cannot choose, order the mixed box. That is what it is for.

Allergens. Everything is baked in a home kitchen that regularly handles wheat, eggs, dairy, walnuts, hazelnuts, almonds and poppy seed. Katka cannot guarantee any item is free from traces of these. Tell her about allergies when you enquire and she will be straight with you about what is and is not safe to order.
