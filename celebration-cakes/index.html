Sizes and serves

Cake sizes are easier to picture in slices than in inches. These are generous dessert portions — if the cake is the only sweet thing at the party, size up.

  • 6″ round — 8 to 10 slices from $95
  • 8″ round — 16 to 20 slices from $135
  • 10″ round — 28 to 32 slices from $185
  • Two tier (6″ + 8″) — 35 to 40 from $280
  • Slab tray — 24 squares plech from $120

Prices are a starting point. Detailed modelling, fresh flowers, gold leaf or multiple fillings add to the quote.

Allergens. Everything is baked in a home kitchen that regularly handles wheat, eggs, dairy, walnuts, hazelnuts, almonds and poppy seed. Katka cannot guarantee any item is free from traces of these. Tell her about allergies when you enquire and she will be straight with you about what is and is not safe to order.

How it works

Ordering, step by step

1. Tell Katka

Send the date, the occasion, roughly how many people and any flavour you have your heart set on.

2. Agree the design

Katka replies with options, a sketch if it helps, and a firm price. Nothing is locked in until you are happy.

3. Confirm with a deposit

A 30% deposit holds your date. Popular weekends fill up several weeks ahead.

4. Collect it fresh

Your order is baked in the two days before pickup and handed over boxed, with serving notes.

Let’s bake

Let’s design your cake

Send the date and the occasion and Katka will come back with flavour ideas and a price. Two weeks’ notice is ideal, but do ask about short notice.
